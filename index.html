    The story

    Five movements of kept things

    A prologue and four acts, moving from a manuscript under a paper crane to a final fold in November light. Every chapter keeps its own object, its own minute, its own weather.

    The soundtrack

    Twenty-one generative palettes

    One live WebAudio palette per chapter. Press play here, or begin a chapter and the reader carries its score with you.

    The galleries

    Plates at the end of chapters

    Each chapter closes with two original plates: a bloom study and a relic study. Fan-art uploads are not part of this build yet, but the gallery pages are already in place.

    Coming later

    Artwork uploads are not part of this build yet. When they arrive, files will be stored locally with IndexedDB — never uploaded, never tracked.
